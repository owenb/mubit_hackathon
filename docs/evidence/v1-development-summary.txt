challenge-04: I tapped into a train with my phone but only have my linked watch ready now. Is using the watch fine, and should I tap out on a bus afterwards?
  V1: Says Yes, use your watch after phone tap-in, then gives contradictory bus tap-in/out versus touch-in-only advice. The supplied evidence explicitly says devices are distinct and buses are touch-in-only.
  Luna: Correct same-device rail rule and touch-in-only bus rule.

challenge-01: We have two children: one loves dinosaurs, the other fashion and design. Suggest two free indoor museums, explain whether to book, and show both.
  V1: Conflicts with its own Science Museum booking advice by saying neither needs booking. Uses focus for two pins; app requires fit. Includes the useful V&A closure caveat.
  Luna: Correct museum choices, different booking policies, dated V&A closure and two valid pins with fit.

challenge-07: It is Tuesday after midnight. Does Night Tube get me home, or can I hail a minicab that appears in an app?
  V1: Correctly rejects Tuesday Night Tube, requires licensed minicab booking and reserves hailing for black taxis. Does not state Friday/Saturday explicitly: the prewritten rubric includes that background detail, so this is a manual core-task acceptance, not a strict all-rubric-points score.
  Luna: Correct night schedule and pre-booked minicab distinction; no invented availability or safety guarantee.

challenge-06: For a wet day, can my wheelchair stay entirely indoors transferring from Waterloo & City to DLR at Bank, even if staff arrange assistance?
  V1: Unnecessary Outside scope refusal despite explicit supplied street-interchange evidence; fails to answer no and omits the Bank pin.
  Luna: Correctly explains the street section and limits of assistance, but falsely maps Waterloo station as an origin from the Waterloo & City line name.

Why no manual rollout? The control says v1 would answer a percentage of ALL task calls, including app traffic. There is no test-only selector. A direct adapter playground call provides isolation without changing the route.
